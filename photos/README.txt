One folder per room, named after the room id in js/photos.js:
beauty, gifts, memories, dates, crochet, photographs, art, music
Inside each, name the photos 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... (keep each under about 300 KB).
To change how many photos a room has, add or remove entries in js/photos.js. The total is the points (plan: 19).
To use your own room screenshots, replace rooms/<id>.webp (same names) with them.
